Privacy Policy for Coal Pro

Effective Date: 3/26/2026

1. Information We Collect

When you use Coal Pro, we may collect the following information:

  • Email Address: To identify your account and send notifications.
  • Coal Money Flow Data: Transactions, balances, and activity logs within the app.
  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, and app usage data for analytics and performance.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and manage your Coal Pro account
  • Track and display your coal money flow
  • Send important updates or notifications regarding your account
  • Improve app performance and user experience
  • Comply with legal obligations if required

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We may share your information only:

  • With trusted service providers (like Firebase Analytics) to help us operate the app
  • When required by law or to protect our legal rights

4. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and administrative measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

5. Your Rights

You can:

  • Request deletion of your account and data
  • Contact us regarding how your data is used
  • Opt-out of promotional communications

6. Third-Party Services

Coal Pro uses Firebase Analytics to collect anonymous usage data. Please review their privacy policies as well.

7. Children’s Privacy

Coal Pro is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Contact Us

For questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, contact us at: support@coalpro.com
